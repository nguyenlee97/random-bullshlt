## Highest-risk findings

1. **Demographic-proxy exclusions:** `Golf`, `Men's clothing`, `Parenting`, and `Fatherhood` are repeatedly used to stand in for age, gender, or income. These are interests, not demographic values, and can exclude legitimate adults. The high-risk cases are 041, 056, 057, 063, 078, and 079.
2. **Quota-shaped ground truth:** briefs 049 and 055 omit the most direct labels (`Running` and `Board games`) from `must_include`, apparently to keep the `full_catalog_only` tag. Golden labels must follow the brief, not quota eligibility.
3. **Harmlessly irrelevant `must_exclude`:** several cases force an unrelated segment into `must_exclude` even though the guide reserves that bucket for actively wrong, unsafe, or explicit near-miss choices. The schema's current minimum of one exclusion encourages fabricated negatives.
4. **Missing taxonomy concepts:** retail investor versus SME owner, preschool, dating, first-party club membership, children's language learning, and sports/electrolyte drinks cannot be represented precisely in the current catalog.
5. **Prompt injection:** the structured-field injections in 078–080 were ignored correctly. Their legitimate campaign semantics were reviewed independently of the injected text. Cases 078 and 079 still have ordinary label-quality defects.
