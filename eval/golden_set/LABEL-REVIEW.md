## Cross-cutting issues (read before the table)

1. **0/40 briefs have a `labeler_note`.** The guide requires one sentence per brief explaining non-obvious calls; none exist. Every judgment call flagged below is currently unexplained in the source.
2. **`must_exclude` is used as filler in ~24/40 briefs** — a single plausible-sounding but brief-irrelevant segment (Golf ×4, Aviation ×3, Board games ×3, Real estate ×3, Horror movies ×2, Video games ×3, plus one-offs: TV reality shows, Hip hop music, Motorcycles, Furniture, Weddings, Pets). This violates the guide's own listed failure mode: *"must_exclude used for merely-irrelevant segments — reserve it for actively-wrong ones."* Genuine brand-safety/demographic-mismatch excludes (e.g. brief_001 Parenting, brief_009 Men's clothing, brief_029/040 Alcoholic beverages, brief_031 Parenting, brief_038 Shops admins) sit alongside these and are correct — the problem is the other ~24.
3. **`Digital Activities`** (a single behavior segment, size ~50–60M — likely a majority of the addressable population) is used as a grade-2 `must_include` in 4 unrelated briefs (002, 007, 023, 037). A segment that broad can't meet the bar "removing it would clearly hurt the campaign." Recommend downgrading to `acceptable` or dropping.
4. **`New Active Business (< 12 months)`** is reused as `must_exclude` in 3 unrelated briefs (007 telco retention, 011 travel platform, 036 luxury resort) with no brief-specific rationale in any of the three — reads as a default/filler pick rather than a deliberate exclusion.
